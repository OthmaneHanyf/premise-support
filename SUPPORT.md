# Premise support

Last updated: 2026-11-25.

Premise is an app for Jira that records the assumptions behind a work item, facts to check and decisions to make, and holds the work until they are settled.

## Getting help

Open an issue in this repository. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened. Do not paste ticket text, names or anything confidential: describe it instead.

## Before you write

- The Premise panel is not on a work item until someone adds it. Open the work item's apps menu and choose Premise. A Jira admin can show it on all work items.
- Outside agents can reach Premise only after an admin switches on its tools for the site in Atlassian Administration, under the site's connected apps.
- The gate works only on a transition that carries the Premise rule. A Jira admin adds the rule in the workflow editor.
