# Premise privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25.

Premise is an app for Jira. It records the assumptions behind a work item: facts to check and decisions to make.

## What Premise stores

- The text of each assumption, question, option, answer and piece of evidence that people or agents add on a work item.
- The Atlassian account id of the person who added an entry, owns a decision or answered it. Premise stores the id only, not the name, email address or picture.
- The key of the work item an entry belongs to, and the time of each change.
- The app's settings for your site.

Premise does not store passwords, tokens or payment details.

## Where it is stored

Everything is kept in Atlassian's own storage for Forge apps, inside your site's installation of Premise. Premise has no servers of its own and sends your data to no other service.

## Who can see it

People who can already see a work item in Jira can see the Premise entries on it. Premise checks Jira's permission for every read and every change.

## Agents and AI

Premise makes no AI model calls of its own. If you use it with Rovo, or connect an outside coding agent to it, that agent acts under your own account, sees only what you may see, and is covered by its own provider's terms. Premise records what the agent adds, as described above.

## Keeping and deleting

- You can remove an entry in the app at any time.
- When a work item is deleted, Premise removes its entries.
- When an Atlassian account is closed, Premise removes that account id from its records.
- When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the app's stored data under its rules for Forge apps.
